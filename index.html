      How to practise

      1. Learn the chords. Pick a key, press Play, and follow the highlighted bar, switching chords when it changes. Start slow (60–70 BPM).
      2. Learn Box 1. Switch to Scale notes, choose Box 1, and play up and down the notes of that shape over the band.
      3. Make phrases. Pick two or three notes, repeat a short rhythm, and finish on the orange root note. Use the blue note as a passing note, never somewhere to rest.
      4. Follow the changes. When the chord moves to IV or V, aim for that chord's notes (switch to Chord notes to see them).
